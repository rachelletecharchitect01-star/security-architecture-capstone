# Capstone Defense Deck Outline

Target: 12–15 slides plus backup slides.

1. **Title** — project title, student, program, date
2. **Executive takeaway** — one-sentence decision and the conditions supporting it
3. **Business problem** — why security investment decisions are difficult
4. **Decision and scope** — investment, decision-maker, perspective, time horizon
5. **Research questions** — primary and supporting questions
6. **Evidence base** — qualified public sources and their roles
7. **Analytical framework** — risk-to-financial-value logic
8. **Methods** — variables, scenarios, equations, and validation
9. **Descriptive findings** — the evidence before the ROI model
10. **Base-case results** — ROI, NPV, payback, and expected benefit
11. **Uncertainty** — scenario and sensitivity results
12. **Break-even analysis** — thresholds that change the decision
13. **Recommendation** — action, conditions, and implementation considerations
14. **Limitations** — what the study cannot establish
15. **Conclusion** — concise answer to the research question

## Backup slides

- Detailed source table
- Variable dictionary
- Full equations
- Assumption ranges
- Additional sensitivity results
- Data-quality diagnostics
- Reproducibility workflow

## Presentation principles

- Lead with the decision, not the software.
- Use one main message per slide.
- Distinguish observed data from assumptions visually.
- Display uncertainty with every decision-critical estimate.
- Avoid causal language unless the design supports it.
